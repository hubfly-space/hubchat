# Code of Conduct

HubChat is built by people who may have different backgrounds, identities, and
levels of experience. Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep technical
disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

Report a conduct concern privately to `conduct@hubchat.dev`. Maintainers may
remove harmful content, pause participation, or ban accounts when necessary to
keep the project safe and constructive.
